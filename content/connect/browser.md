## Open the chat

Go to [ircx.io/chats](https://ircx.io/chats), or choose **Chats** in the site header, and pick a room.

## As a guest or with an account

Signed out, you chat as a guest under a random guest nickname. Signed in, you chat as your account, under its nickname.

Signing out ends your account's chat instead of carrying it on as a guest. To chat as a guest, open the chat again.

See [Accounts and guests](../about/accounts-and-guests.md) for more on the difference.
