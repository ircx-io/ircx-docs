## Guests

Anyone can chat as a guest, with no account. On the chat server, a guest's nickname is shown with `>` in front of it.

## Accounts

Create an account at [ircx.io/account/register](https://ircx.io/account/register), then verify your e-mail address to activate it.

When you first sign in, you choose a nickname before you can use the rest of the site.
