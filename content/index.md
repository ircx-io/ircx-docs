ircx.io is a chat service whose rooms you can join in a web browser or with an IRC client. The server speaks IRC as [RFC 1459](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc1459.html) describes it, along with the [IRCX extensions](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-pfenning-irc-extensions-04) and the dialects MSN Chat clients use.

## Where to start

- [Connecting](./connect/index.md) shows how the service is laid out and how to reach a room from a browser or an IRC client.
- [Protocol notes](./protocol/index.md) are for people who write or configure IRC clients.
- [About the service](./about/index.md) covers accounts, guests and the legal pages.
